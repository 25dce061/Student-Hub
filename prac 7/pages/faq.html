FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about the Student Hub Portal, academic courses, exams, fees, and campus events.

General
What is Student Hub?

Student Hub is an integrated university portal designed for CHARUSAT students, providing instantaneous access to academic schedules, real-time attendance tracking, exam results, fee payments, student profiles, and campus events.

Login & Security
How can I login to Student Hub?

Navigate to the Login page from the main navigation menu and enter your registered student username (e.g., yesha) and password. For default testing accounts, use password 1234.

Login & Security
How do I reset my password if forgotten?

Click on the "Forgot Password?" link on the Login page or go to Settings > Change Password to securely update your portal credentials.

Account & Profile
How can I register as a new student?

Click on the Register option in the navigation menu and fill out your full name, student username, registered university email, and a secure password.

Attendance
Where can I check my attendance percentage?

Open the Attendance section from your Student Dashboard. You can review course-by-course attendance status, lecture/lab breakdown, and compliance alerts.

Examinations
Where can I view my examination results and grades?

Select the Result card on your Student Dashboard to view semester marks, grade point averages, subject credits, and examination qualification status.

Fees & Payments
Where can I check my semester fee details?

Open the Fees section to view total tuition fees, scholarship deductions, received amounts, and outstanding balances.

Events
Where can I find and register for university events?

Visit the Events page to browse technical hackathons, cultural festivals, workshops, sports tournaments, and conferences. You can filter by category, sort by date, and click "View Details" to open the interactive event popup modal!

Account & Profile
How can I preview or update my Student ID profile?

Visit the Profile page to edit contact information, view your enrolled department, and click "Preview ID Card" to generate an instant verified digital student identity card.

Support
How can I contact the Student Hub Helpdesk?

You can visit our Contact page for university helpline numbers (+91 985641237), official email (studenthub@gmail.com), or submit an online query using the help button below.

Still Have a Question?

If you cannot find the answer to your question, reach out directly to the Student Hub Helpdesk.

Submit a Question to Helpdesk
